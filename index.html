How to read it

Disc area is the share of the window spent in that behaviour. A disc filling the whole ring limit would mean 100% of the time.
Clock ring: one arc per 5-min slice, starting at 0′ at the top and running clockwise. Darker arcs mean a bigger share of that slice. Faded arcs fall outside the window.
Arrow A → B: B came right after A. Thickness = transitions per learner.
Loop: the behaviour followed itself.

About this tool

Everything runs in your browser. The log you paste is never uploaded. The page remembers your last log on this device so it is there next time; use pseudonymous learner IDs and clear it when you are done with real student data.

The link logic is adapted from ordered network analysis (ONA). This is a descriptive figure, not a full ONA: it does not normalise, reduce dimensions or test differences between groups. For statistical comparisons, run ONA itself.
